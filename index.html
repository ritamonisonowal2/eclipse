How it works

Explainable checks

Four checks. Zero guesswork.

Every analysis report shows the exact computer vision signals that influenced the final score.

Document Validation

Aspect-ratio fit, focus sharpness, OCR text density, and standard layout geometry — confirms the document structure matches known formats.

Tampering & Editing Analysis

Scans for compression hotspots, cloned pixel regions, spliced text boxes, and inconsistent digital noise signatures across the document surface.

Face & Selfie Verification

Detects document portrait bounding boxes, generates 128-d facial embeddings, and compares vector distance against the live selfie capture.

Data Coherence & Cross-Checks

Ensures dates of birth, issue dates, document numbers, and MRZ checksum strings are mathematically plausible and internally consistent.
